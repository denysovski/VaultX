Terms of sale

The rules for buying from VAULTX, written to be read.

Orders

An order is confirmed when you see the confirmation page and receive the receipt email. We may cancel and fully refund an order if a piece sells out before it is packed.

Prices and payment

Prices are in euro and include VAT. Shipping is shown before you pay. Card payments are taken when the order is placed; bank transfers must arrive within 3 days.

Delivery

Orders leave Prague within one working day. Delivery windows on the product and checkout pages are estimates from the courier. Duties outside the EU are paid by the recipient.

Returns and refunds

You can return unworn pieces within 30 days of delivery, as described on the help desk. EU customers keep their statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on top of this.

Warranty

Every piece carries the statutory 24-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear, fading of garment-dyed colours and damage from ignoring the care label are not defects.

Problems

Late, damaged or missing parcels: report them here within 7 days of the expected delivery date.
