FAQ

Where do you ship?

Everywhere in the EU, plus the UK, Switzerland, Norway and the US. Orders leave our Prague warehouse within one working day.

How much is shipping?

Standard delivery is €8, express €18 and pickup point €4. Standard delivery is free on orders over €200.

Will I pay customs?

Not inside the EU. Orders to the UK, Switzerland, Norway and the US may be charged import duty by the courier on delivery.

How do returns work?

You have 30 days from delivery. Start a return from this page, print the label and drop the parcel at any pickup point. Refunds land within 5 working days of us receiving it.

My parcel shows delivered but I do not have it

Check with neighbours and your building reception first, then report it below within 7 days. We open a claim with the courier and send a replacement or refund once it is confirmed.

How do your sizes run?

Hoodies and outerwear are boxy, tees are regular. Every product page has measurements and a note on how the model wears it.
